It’s been several months since [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/) transitioned to become part of Digital Scholar, and we wanted to take a moment to update the community on what the Interim Steering Committee has been working on since then — and what’s still ahead.

### Progress on the Ground

**Translation work is underway.** We’re actively developing a Greek translation of the statements, adding to the growing list of languages in which the [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/) vocabulary is available. This work reflects our ongoing commitment to making rights statements accessible to cultural heritage institutions and their users worldwide, regardless of language.

**The website now features a language switcher,** allowing users to view [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/) and its statements in the 14 available languages. This long-requested feature lays the groundwork for future translation updates.

### Building the Governance Foundation

The Steering Committee has focused much of its recent efforts on **drafting bylaws to establish a new governance structure** for RightsStatements.org under Digital Scholar’s fiscal sponsorship.

This work is guided by a number of key priorities:

* **Equitable representation.** We are evaluating governance models that ensure broad, representative participation from the diverse community of institutions and practitioners who use and contribute to the statements.
* **Fiscal sustainability.** As part of Digital Scholar’s fiscal sponsorship, we are working to ensure [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/) has a stable and sustainable financial foundation for the long term.
* **Global reach.** Because the statements are used internationally, maintaining global participation and accessibility is central to our governance model.

### Why Governance First

We know there’s community interest in updates to the statements themselves, and we hear that. However, we seek to be transparent and believe that **before we can effectively take on substantive updates to the statements, we need governance re-established.** A clear, representative decision-making structure assures that any future changes to the statements are made thoughtfully, with the right voices at the table, and with a process the community can trust. That’s the work happening right now, and it’s the necessary groundwork for everything that will follow.

### We Want to Hear From You

The value of the statements lies in their use, and we welcome your stories. How does your organization use [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/)? Whether for rights and reuse workflows, integration with digital collections platforms, or unique institutional applications, we invite your input.

Please send your impact stories and use cases to **rights-statements@digitalscholar.org**. We may feature selected stories in future updates to highlight the community’s real-world impact.

Thank you for your sustained patience and engagement as we build a stronger foundation for [RightsStatements.org](http://rightsstatements.org/)’s future.
